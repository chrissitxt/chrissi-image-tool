Pick an image, drag it to move it and use the slider or the mouse wheel to zoom. The bright frame is what gets exported. Everything runs in your browser on a canvas, nothing gets uploaded.

Because the image is redrawn, all metadata like camera info and GPS is dropped automatically. The size shown updates live while you change the format or quality, so you can stay under a platform's limit.
